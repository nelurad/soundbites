# SoundBites

Sound you can touch: playable instruments and hands-on music lessons by Nelu Radpour (Florida State University).
A sister site to [VisualStims](https://nelurad.github.io/visualstims/) (brain science to look at) and [NeuroSims](https://nelurad.github.io/neurosims/) (brain science to learn).

Live at https://nelurad.github.io/soundbites/

Each piece is a folder with an `index.html` and a 900×600 `thumb.jpg`, listed on the root `index.html`. All sound is synthesised live with Web Audio; there are no recordings.

## Instruments

| | Piece | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| a | [Rosewood](rosewood/) | A xylophone you can actually play: modal bar synthesis (strike position changes the tone; near the cord goes dead), harder/faster hits are louder and brighter, hold to roll, drag for a glissando, multi-touch, computer-keyboard play, hard/rubber/yarn mallets, marimba mode, room echo, and a learn-a-tune mode. Web Audio + canvas. |
| b | [Santoor](santoor/) | A Persian santur to play: 72 strings in 18 courses of four, brass bass on the right bridges, steel on the left bridges whose short side rings an octave up. Each course is four slightly detuned Karplus–Strong strings (the shimmer), strike position changes the tone, untouched strings ring in sympathy, hold for riz (tremolo), wood or felt mezrabs, and the dastgah tunings Shur, Mahur, Homayun, Segah and Chahargah with koron notes. A *listen* button plays a short phrase in the current tuning. Web Audio + canvas. |

## Lessons

| No. | Lesson | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Split the Octave](split-the-octave/) | Music theory from square one, as a series of nine playable lessons with live sound: what a sound is, tone colour (timbre), naming the notes, sharps & flats, beat & rhythm, intervals, major & minor, chords, and *Why twelve?* (harmonics, the comma, 12-TET). Shared Web Audio kit in `split-the-octave/kit/`. |
